# Competitor USP analysis → Pravaah marketing brief

> Research date: 2026-07-25  
> Purpose: Inform public SaaS marketing site positioning (independent of any consumer salon brand).

## Global leaders — claimed USPs

| Vendor | Homepage USP pillars | ICP | Pricing signal | Weak vs Pravaah |
|--------|----------------------|-----|----------------|-----------------|
| **Zenoti** | AI workforce (receptionist, marketer…); single OS; plug revenue leaks; 30k businesses | Enterprise / franchise | Opaque / sales-led | Heavy, expensive, slow rollout for 3–20 branch India chains |
| **Mindbody** | Marketplace demand; payments; marketing; multi-location | Fitness + wellness | Sales-led | Fitness DNA; not India salon-first |
| **Booksy** | Marketplace; 24/7 booking; all features in every plan | Solo / SMB | ~$30/mo + boost fee | Marketplace tax; weak India compliance |
| **Fresha** | Consumer marketplace scale; booking + POS | Solo → SMB | Low seat + marketplace fees | Commission economics; thin GST/UPI story |
| **Phorest** | Retention SMS ROI; loyalty; branded app | Premium EU/AU/US | Quote-led | Expensive; not India-native |
| **Boulevard** | Precision Scheduling; beauty-native POS; CX | Premium US | Demo-led | US only |
| **Vagaro** | All-in-one + marketplace; scale stats | Solo → multi | Promo ~$24/mo | Breadth over India depth |
| **Square Appointments** | Free booking site; payments ecosystem | Horizontal SMB | Free + processing | Not salon-ops deep |

## Clichés to avoid (and we avoided)

- “All-in-one salon software to grow your business”
- Empty “more revenue / more clients / more growth”
- Unqualified “#1 platform”
- Feature dump as the story
- Generic “AI-powered” without a job-to-be-done
- Marketplace discovery as the hero for chains that already have footfall

## Pravaah differentiators (shipped — marketable now)

1. **Multi-branch command centre** — CEO overview across outlets  
2. **Branch P&L** — revenue vs salary/rent/product/misc with sync  
3. **Market Pulse** — in-brand rank + anonymised peer cohorts + playbooks  
4. **Manager floor PWA** — walk-in → GST → UPI/Cash/Card → WhatsApp receipt  
5. **India GST depth** — per-branch GSTIN, CGST/SGST PDF invoices  
6. **WhatsApp campaigns included** (Growth story) — visit/spend audience filters  
7. **Geofenced verified attendance** — selfie + GPS + leave  
8. **Inventory movements** at branch  
9. **Leads CRM** from public enquiries  
10. **Audit-ready discounts / payments**  
11. **10 India locales** in product UI  
12. **Anti-marketplace** — keep 100% of bookings  

**Do not claim yet:** customer self-booking app, Google Reserve, review gating, Razorpay, AI receptionist, loyalty wallets.

## Messaging used on this site

**Positioning:**  
*The operating system for multi-branch salon chains in India — GST billing, branch P&L, WhatsApp campaigns, and a manager floor app in one continuous flow.*

**Hero:** Brand-first (Pravaah) → “Run every salon like your best one.”

**Conversion demo path:** Walk-in GST → Branch P&L → WhatsApp campaign → Market Pulse.

**Explicit constraints for this site:**
- Independent SaaS brand — no consumer salon brand references  
- No “society / RWA / apartment” messaging  
- Transparent mid-market pricing vs Zenoti opacity / Fresha commission anxiety
